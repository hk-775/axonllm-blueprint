# Code of conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project.

Contributors should:

- communicate with empathy and technical clarity
- critique ideas and code, not people
- respect privacy and avoid sharing confidential material
- accept constructive feedback and correct mistakes

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that violate these expectations.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security or maintainer contact channels.
